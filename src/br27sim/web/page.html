      Shutdown system (IC.9)

      Tap a switch to open it. The Primary Master Switch cuts the battery directly. The Cockpit Main Switch, BOTS and inertia switch sit in series with the kill relay coil, whose contacts feed the injectors, coils and fuel pump. No BSPD: that is only required with electronic throttle, and BR27 runs a cable.

        CAN bus and data logging

        One CAN 2.0 bus with the EMU Black at one end and the dash MCU at the other. The four corner nodes sit on short stubs along the way, and the dash MCU reads the IMU and GPS directly and logs them, so they stay off the bus.             Fault codes and CAN priority

            Every node runs the same logic for each code it owns: a condition has to hold for its debounce time before the code goes active, and it has to stay clear for its clear time before it resets. Latching codes stay active until the car is reset. Fault frames get the lowest IDs on the bus, so they win arbitration over all sensor and ECU data.

            Fault to test

            The frame is injected at the busiest moment of the simulated bus, when the most frames are queued, so it has to win arbitration to get through.

            Powertrain wiring

            EMU Black pins from the label on the team's unit, with a proposed use for each one on the CBR600RR, and the harness from the battery through the firewall to the engine. The EMU only switches the ground side of the injectors, coils and pump relay. Their +12 V comes from the kill bus, so the shutdown switches kill fuel and spark in hardware.
